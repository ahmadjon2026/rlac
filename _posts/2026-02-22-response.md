## Introduction
For Assignment 1, I analyzed three canonical Harry Potter novels by J.K. Rowling and three fanfiction stories set in the same universe. I focused on how language around authority and agency differs between the two groups. And, for the Response 1, to interpret the visuals, I used Perplexity AI in two separate chats. This was deliberate so the AI would not have prior context, allowing independent interpretations for each set of visuals.

The first chat focused on the bar chart and heatmap, which I sent together as two visuals. I asked four questions about them. The second chat focused on the Voyant Trends line chart, where I asked four additional questions about word frequency trends for plan, lead, decide, and control.

## Part 1: Bar Chart and Heatmap
Questions I asked in this chat (first four questions only):
```

2. What patterns appear in canonical versus fanfiction texts?
```
